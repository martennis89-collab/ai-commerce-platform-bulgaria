# Amboras Design Taste

Use this as the taste compass for Amboras.

## Desired Feel

Amboras should feel:

- Premium but practical.
- Local to Bulgarian commerce without becoming folkloric or decorative.
- Calm, focused, and trustworthy.
- Warm through language, service, and merchant usefulness, not beige default styling.
- Sophisticated enough for real merchants, simple enough for small business owners.
- Distinct from generic Shopify clone, generic SaaS dashboard, and generic AI website.

## Design Lens

When making or reviewing design decisions, ask:

- Would a merchant trust this with their shop?
- Can a tired owner understand the screen quickly?
- Does the page make the product or merchant shine?
- Is the interface doing real work, or just looking decorated?
- Does this still feel like Amboras?

## Brand Boundaries

Do not drift into:

- Tech startup coldness.
- Overly playful creator-tool energy.
- Marketplace clutter.
- Banking-app stiffness.
- Template-store sameness.
- AI-generated "premium" tropes: giant hero text, blurred gradient backgrounds, glass cards, endless identical cards, and vague copy.

## Output Standard

If the design is not good enough, say so directly and propose a smaller, better direction. Prefer one strong visual idea over many decorative details.
