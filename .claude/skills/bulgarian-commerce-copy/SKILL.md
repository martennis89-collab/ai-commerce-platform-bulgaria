# Bulgarian Commerce Copy

Use this for Amboras copywriting and copy review.

## Voice

Write clear, natural Bulgarian commerce copy. It should feel useful to Bulgarian small and medium merchants, not translated from English SaaS copy.

Prefer:

- Specific, concrete language.
- Short sentences.
- Merchant/customer clarity.
- Trust, practicality, and warmth.
- Bulgarian first when the UI is merchant- or shopper-facing for the Bulgarian market.

Avoid:

- Invented product claims.
- Invented prices, stock, discounts, certifications, origins, ingredients, warranties, or delivery promises.
- Vague hype such as "premium experience", "seamless journey", or "unlock your potential".
- Literal English structure translated into Bulgarian.
- Overly formal bureaucratic phrasing.

## Generated Product Drafts

When facts are missing:

- Mark fields as draft or unknown.
- Ask for merchant confirmation.
- Use placeholders only if the product schema explicitly supports them.
- Never turn uncertain information into published catalogue data.

## Review Checklist

- Is it true based on supplied facts?
- Would a Bulgarian merchant or shopper understand it immediately?
- Does it help the user act?
- Is the tone Amboras: calm, trustworthy, useful?
- Are risky claims avoided or flagged?
