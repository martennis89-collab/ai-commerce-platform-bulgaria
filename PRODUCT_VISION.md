# Product Vision

## Promise

**Разкажи ни какво продаваш. След няколко минути имаш истински онлайн магазин.**

The output is not a landing page. It is a functioning ecommerce business: storefront, catalogue, cart, checkout, orders, inventory, shipping, payment options, transactional email, domain, legal/profile surfaces, and an AI operator that can safely change the store.

## Customer

Small Bulgarian makers, sourcers, and social sellers of physical products who currently rely on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Messenger, Viber, or word of mouth and do not want to learn ecommerce infrastructure.

## Experience

The mental model is `intent → AI action → visible result`. A few natural prompts, photos, and facts create a useful preview quickly. The merchant sees live work, can correct it conversationally, and can publish only after readiness checks. AI suggestions are facts-aware: merchant-confirmed facts, AI inferences, and recommendations remain distinct.

## Product principles

- Make opinionated safe decisions for the merchant.
- Show work and preserve the conversation during long runs.
- Keep draft, preview, and live states separate.
- Make every meaningful AI action auditable and reversible where possible.
- Hide infrastructure complexity without hiding important consequences.
- Build real commerce before adding breadth.

## Success

The north-star outcome is active stores generating genuine monthly orders. Activation, order completion, retention, and willingness to pay matter more than the number of generated stores.
